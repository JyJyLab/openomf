{SIZE 6}{SPACING 7}{COLOR:DEFAULT}Now it's time for the real test!

   The red energy meter at the top shows how much damage your robot can take before shutdown. Every successful strike against you lowers that total. If your energy meter dips below zero, your robot collapses.

   The thin blue bar shows your stun damage. This bar flashes when dangerously low. When it drops below zero, your pilot becomes dizzy and loses control for several seconds.

   Some arenas contain dangerous hazards: fireballs, spikes, fighter jets, and the like. If you can't take the heat, you can turn them off in the GAMEPLAY menu.

Title: Help Page 10
Data: {CENTER OFF}
{SHADOWS ON}{SIZE 8}{COLOR:YELLOW}Offense{COLOR:DEFAULT}

{SIZE 6}{SPACING 7}{COLOR:DEFAULT}   Robots have a wide range of attacks. Before you can pommel your opponent with them, you must know the controls. You can perform any move with either the keyboard or a joystick.

"Forward" means towards your opponent.
"Back" means away from your opponent.

   The attacks use these directions along with the kick and PUNCH buttons. punching while pressing back will produce a different move than punching while pressing forward.

   Press up to jump. To jump higher and farther,  first press down then press up.
{CENTER ON}

ID: 10
Title: Help Page 11
Data: {CENTER OFF}
{SHADOWS ON}{SIZE 8}{COLOR:YELLOW}Defense

{SIZE 6}{SPACING 7}{COLOR:DEFAULT}   To defend yourself against an attack while you're on the ground, press back without attacking (don't punch or kick). Your robot will block any type of move, except leg sweeps.

   To block leg sweeps, press down-back. Pressing down-back will not block air attacks.

   While airborne, you cannot defend any attack. Jump with caution.
{CENTER ON}

Title: Help Page 12

{SHADOWS ON}{SIZE 8}{COLOR:YELLOW}Playing Tips{COLOR:DEFAULT}

{SIZE 6}{SPACING 7}   Each pilot gives a robot an entirely new feel. Try several pilots and choose one that best suits your abilities. In a one player game, the computer will play the other pilots, using their individual personalities to guide the intelligence of the robots they wield.

   Each robot has several special moves which are both undocumented and very powerful. To perform a special move, you need to combine controller movements and button presses in rapid succession. Watch the computer play to see some of the special moves, then try to do them yourself.

Example: Perform Jaguar's Concussion Cannon by pressing down-back-punch.
